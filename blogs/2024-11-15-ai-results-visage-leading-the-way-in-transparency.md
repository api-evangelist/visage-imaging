As AI-generated measurements and segmentations (e.g., AI Results) have become more commonplace, the need arises to better inform the reporting radiologist that an AI algorithm was involved in the imaging under interpretation. This helps raise awareness that it was an AI algorithm rather than a peer radiologist who created the measurement (and/or AI Result(s); bolsters liability and regulatory protection (Software as a Medical Device [SaMD]); and facilitates data curation for research purposes, among other benefits.
