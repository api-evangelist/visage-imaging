SIIM 2025 ("SIIM25") was another record-setting year for Visage! We experienced tremendous customer interest (especially on day one) despite a drop-off in attendance after day two (due to Memorial Day weekend). What was record-setting this year was the number of presentations and posters presented that included our customers, staff and technology.
