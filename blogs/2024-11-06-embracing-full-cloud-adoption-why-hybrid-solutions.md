I want to address a critical topic that some healthcare organizations are contemplating: the misconception that adding on-premises servers to cloud deployments (e.g., so called “hybrid” cloud) enhances security and availability. In reality, this hybrid approach often introduces more risks than benefits. For the past four years, all of Visage's new installations of the Visage 7 Enterprise Imaging Platform (e.g., Visage 7 CloudPACS) have been cloud-based.
